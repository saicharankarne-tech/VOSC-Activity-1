📖 Ludo Classic - Rules & How to Play

  • 🎯 Objective: Be the first player to guide all your pawns from the Yard, clockwise around the track, and into the Center Home.
  • 🎲 Starting a Pawn: You must roll a 6 to move a pawn from your Yard onto your colored Start tile.
  • ⭐ Safe Zones: Starting tiles and tiles marked with a star (★) are Safe Zones. Pawns located on safe zones cannot be captured!
  • ⚔️ Capturing: When your pawn lands on a non-safe tile occupied by an opponent's pawn, the opponent's pawn is captured and sent back to their Yard. Capturing also rewards you with a Bonus Roll!
  • 🎉 Rolling a Six: Rolling a 6 grants you an extra roll!
  • 🏁 Home Stretch: Once around the board, your pawns enter your colored Home Column. An exact roll is needed to enter the Center Home Triangle.
  • 🤖 Play Modes: Play with 2, 3, or 4 players. You can toggle each player between Human and Bot AI anytime!
